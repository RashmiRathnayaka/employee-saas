Project Proposal: Customizable Employee Management Mobile App & SaaS Platform
1. Project Overview
We are planning to develop a comprehensive Employee Management Platform that combines a mobile application for employees, a web portal for HR and management, and a main website where companies can purchase the system.

The main objective is to digitize the daily administrative and employee-related activities of companies through a single platform.

Companies will be able to visit our website, select a suitable package, provide their company details, and purchase the system. Based on the information provided, we will configure the platform according to each company's requirements, branding, policies, and organizational structure.

The platform will follow a multi-tenant SaaS architecture, allowing multiple companies to use the same system while keeping their data separate and secure.
2. Employee Mobile Application
Employees will use the mobile application to access their personal employment information and perform daily work-related activities.
Employee Features
- Secure login using an Employee ID and password or another secure authentication method.
- Personal profile containing name, photograph, contact details, department, designation, joining date, and reporting manager.
- View available annual, casual, and sick leave balances.
- Submit leave requests through the application.
- View leave request history and approval status.
- Upload required supporting documents for sick leave.
- View salary details, payslips, allowances, deductions, and payment history according to company permissions.
- Record attendance and working hours.
- Check in when arriving at an assigned external work site.
- Check out when leaving the site.
- View assigned tasks, work schedules, and relevant project information.
- Receive notifications about leave approvals, attendance, salary slips, and company announcements.
- Access a company social feed containing achievements, events, awards, and announcements.
3. HR, Manager, and Admin Web Portal
Different dashboards will be available according to user roles and permissions.
HR Portal
- Create and manage employee profiles.
- Manage departments, designations, and organizational structures.
- Manage leave types, balances, and company leave policies.
- Review leave requests and supporting documents.
- Manage attendance records and reports.
- Manage employee-related documents.
- Access authorized salary and payroll information.
- Publish company announcements and achievements.
- Generate employee, leave, and attendance reports.
Manager Portal
- View employees under their supervision.
- Review and approve or reject leave requests.
- Monitor team attendance and external site visits.
- View site check-in and check-out records.
- Monitor assigned work and team schedules.
- Receive notifications about relevant employee activities.
Company Admin Portal
- Configure company information and branding.
- Manage employee accounts and Employee IDs.
- Assign HR, Manager, and other user roles.
- Configure departments, permissions, and workflows.
- Enable or disable available system modules.
- Configure working hours, leave policies, and site locations.
- Manage company-wide settings and access permissions.
Platform Super Admin Portal
This portal will be managed by our own platform administration team.
- Manage registered companies.
- Monitor subscriptions and payment statuses.
- Manage packages and feature limits.
- Configure and activate company workspaces.
- Monitor platform usage and system health.
- Manage onboarding and customer support.
4. Leave Management System
The leave management module will automate the complete leave process.
1. An employee selects a leave type, dates, and reason.
2. The employee submits the request through the mobile application.
3. The relevant manager or authorized HR officer receives a notification.
4. The request is approved or rejected.
5. The employee receives a notification about the decision.
6. Approved leave records and leave balances are updated automatically.
7. Employees can view their leave history and remaining balance.
Sick Leave Document Management
- Employees can upload medical certificates or other required supporting documents.
- The company can configure the document submission deadline, such as within three days from the start of leave.
- HR can review and verify uploaded documents.
- Document statuses can include Pending Review, Accepted, Rejected, and Additional Information Required.
- Automated reminders can be sent before the configured deadline.
The system should support company-specific policies and authorized exceptions.
5. External Site Visit and Attendance Tracking
This module will be particularly useful for companies whose employees work at customer locations, construction sites, project locations, or other external workplaces.
Site Check-in
- Employees select their assigned work site.
- They record their arrival using a Check-in button.
- The system records the date and time.
- GPS location verification can be enabled when required.
- Managers can receive notifications when employees arrive.
Site Check-out
- Employees record their departure using a Check-out button.
- The system records the departure time and, where enabled, location verification.
- The duration of the visit can be calculated.
- Managers and HR can view site visit history and reports.
The system should use appropriate location permissions, transparent tracking policies, and security controls.
6. Salary and Payroll Management
The payroll module will allow authorized HR or payroll personnel to manage employee salary information.

Features include:
- Basic salary records.
- Allowances and deductions.
- Monthly payslip generation and access.
- Salary payment history and payment status.
- Employee-specific salary visibility permissions.
- Payroll summaries and reports.
The first version may focus on salary records and payslip access, with more advanced payroll calculations introduced later.
7. Company Social Hub
The application will include an internal social platform to improve employee engagement and company communication.

Features include:
- Company achievements and milestones.
- Employee awards and recognitions.
- Company events and celebrations.
- New employee announcements.
- Birthdays and work anniversaries.
- Company news and notices.
- Photos and videos from approved company activities.
- Likes, reactions, and comments, subject to company settings.
- Notifications for important announcements.
HR and authorized administrators will be able to publish and manage content.
8. Notifications and Reports
Notifications
The system can send push notifications, in-app notifications, and emails for:
- Leave request approvals and rejections.
- Pending leave approvals.
- Sick leave document submission reminders.
- Attendance and site check-in reminders.
- Salary slip availability.
- Company announcements and events.
Reports and Analytics
Authorized users will be able to access:
- Total employee count.
- Department-wise employee statistics.
- Daily and monthly attendance reports.
- Leave utilization and remaining balances.
- Pending leave requests.
- External site visit records.
- Salary and payroll summaries, subject to permissions.
- Exportable Excel and PDF reports.
9. Main Website and Company Purchasing Process
We will create a professional website where companies can learn about the platform, explore features, compare packages, and purchase the system.
Company Onboarding Workflow
Step 1: Visit the Website

Companies visit our website and review the platform features, packages, pricing, and demo.

Step 2: Select a Package

Companies choose a suitable package based on employee count and required features.

Step 3: Complete the Company Registration Form

The form may collect:
- Company name and registration details.
- Company address and contact information.
- Company logo and branding preferences.
- Number of employees.
- Departments and organizational structure.
- Working hours and attendance rules.
- Leave types and leave policies.
- Required modules and customization preferences.
- Initial administrator details.
Step 4: Make the Payment

The company completes payment through an integrated payment gateway.

Step 5: Configure the Company Workspace

After payment confirmation, the platform creates or prepares a separate company workspace. Standard settings can be applied automatically, while advanced customizations may require manual review.

Step 6: Activate the Company Account

The company administrator receives access to the management portal and can configure employees, HR users, managers, and company policies.

Step 7: Start Using the System

Employees can log in to the mobile application, while HR and managers use the web portal.
10. Company-Specific Customization
We should develop the platform using a multi-tenant SaaS architecture.

This means that multiple companies will use the same core application, but each company will have its own isolated data, users, configuration, and branding.

Customization options may include:
- Company logo and brand colours.
- Company name and profile.
- Departments and designations.
- Employee ID formats.
- Leave types and leave policies.
- Approval workflows.
- Working hours and attendance rules.
- Site locations and project settings.
- Payroll settings and permissions.
- Company social feed and announcements.
- Enabled modules and subscription limits.
Mobile App Distribution Options
Option A: Shared Mobile Application

All companies use the same mobile application. Employees identify their company through a company code, company-specific login link, or another secure tenant identification method.

Option B: Separate Branded Applications

Premium customers can receive a separately branded application, subject to additional development, testing, deployment, and app-store requirements.

Recommended Approach: Start with one shared mobile application and a multi-tenant backend. Offer separate branded applications as a premium service if commercially justified.

12. Security and Privacy Requirements
The system will handle sensitive employee information, including salary records and medical documents. Security must therefore be a core part of the design.

Required controls include:
- Secure authentication.
- Role-based and company-based access permissions.
- Strict tenant data isolation.
- Encrypted communication.
- Secure storage of passwords and documents.
- Audit logs for sensitive actions.
- Restricted access to salary and medical information.
- Secure file uploads and document access.
- Backup and recovery procedures.
- Appropriate privacy policies and data retention rules.
An Employee ID alone should not be sufficient for authentication. Each user must authenticate securely, and the backend must verify that users can access only the data they are authorized to view.

14. Business Model
The platform can generate revenue through:
- Monthly subscriptions.
- Annual subscriptions.
- Initial setup and customization fees.
- Premium branded mobile applications.
- Additional employee capacity.
- Optional advanced modules and integrations.
A recommended starting model is a setup fee combined with a monthly or annual subscription. Pricing should be determined based on hosting costs, support requirements, employee limits, and the selected features.
15. Final Goal
Our goal is to build a scalable, secure, customizable Employee Management SaaS Platform that enables companies to manage their employees' daily activities through one integrated system.

Instead of maintaining separate manual processes for leave, attendance, site visits, salary information, and company communication, organizations will be able to manage these activities through a centralized web and mobile platform.

The long-term vision is to provide a flexible employee management solution that can serve small businesses, medium-sized companies, and larger organizations with different requirements.

Recommended Starting Point: Design the system architecture, database schema, user roles, and core employee management workflows before developing all the modules. Build the first working version around company registration, employee profiles, leave management, and approval workflows, then expand in phases.
